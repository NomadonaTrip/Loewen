Frequently asked questions

What is a snow windrow?

The dense ridge of snow and ice the city plow leaves across the end of your driveway after it clears the street. It is heavier and harder to move than regular snow because it is packed road snow mixed with ice.

How quickly can you clear my snow windrow?

Within 8 hours of receiving your payment.

What does the service cover?

We clear the ridge of snow left by the city plow across the entrance to your driveway.

Can you handle heavy, compacted snow?

Yes. We have experience clearing the heavy, compacted snow and ice typically left behind by city plows.

Do I need to be home?

No.

What happens if the plow comes back?

If or when the plow comes back, another clearing can be booked.

How much does it cost?

$25 + HST ($28.25 total) per clearing.

Do I need a seasonal contract?

No. Book and pay whenever you need a clearing.

How do I pay?

By one Interac e‑Transfer of $28.25 ($25 + HST) to robl@loewenpropertyworks.ca, with your address in the e‑Transfer notes. That e‑Transfer is your booking and your payment.

Can I get a refund?

We refund $15 if we fail to clear your snow windrow within 8 hours, and give a full refund if we fail to clear it within 24 hours.
